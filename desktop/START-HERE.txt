ILLUSION 1.6.2 - WINDOWS PREVIEW
Private. Customizable. Free.

Open Illusion-0.8.3.exe on Windows 10/11 with .NET Framework 4.8.
A configured download includes its public connection. Existing installs keep
their connection; use your host bot's /connect invitation if needed.
The Cloudflare-hosted connection works while the owner's laptop is off.
Availability depends on Cloudflare, network access and free-plan quotas.
Older laptop-hosted connections still require their host to stay online.

The shared UI catalog includes 39 languages, including Croatian, Serbian,
Bosnian, Bulgarian and Slovak. Illusion is the product name and stays
unchanged across languages.

Usernames: 2-24 Latin letters, numbers, _ and -. Case-insensitive.
Passwords: at least 10 characters. Back up your key in Settings.
Your account password cannot recover chats on another Windows profile.
If you still have this profile's device key, use Forgot password to reset it.
Registration requires separate Terms/Privacy acknowledgements and age 18+.
This adult-only preview has no parental-verification workflow.

Find an exact username. Enter sends; Shift+Enter adds a line.
Ctrl+K finds someone. Ctrl+F searches loaded messages locally.
Right-click a normal message to copy/reply/report it.

+ New group: choose a name and exact usernames (up to 20 people). Every
group message is sealed separately for each current member. Sender names,
replies, unread badges, mute, group activity and a group timer are included.
Attachments, view-once and reactions are disabled in groups. Group names,
membership and join/leave events are visible to the relay. New members cannot
read messages sent before they joined.
Group settings: rename, add a member, change the timer, mute or leave.

+ Photo / Video: attach pictures or short MP4s up to 4 MB. New attachments are
encrypted on your device before upload.
View once: recipient opens once; closing removes this app's opened copy.
Screenshots, recordings and modified clients cannot be prevented.
Profile / Timer: profiles, reports, blocking and disappearing messages.
Auto-delete defaults to OFF in every new chat; timers affect new messages.
Click your own profile picture to edit your picture, banner, bio, font, colors and effect.
Your profile is public to signed-in people who find your username.
Settings customizes your chat font/colors/spacing/artwork/receipts.
Click your bottom-left picture for the live profile preview/editor.
RGB controls include a color plane, channel sliders/numbers and hex input.
Themes change backgrounds/panels/controls; 28 system font names are offered.
Settings -> Privacy and data includes policies and deletion requests.

The Telegram Mini App is served at the relay's /app/ URL. It needs a working
public HTTPS connection before the bot's Open Illusion button can work.
It is a web app inside Telegram and trusts code served by its host.
Restore your .illusionkey to use an existing account in that browser.

Report evidence sharing is optional and unchecked by default. Moderators
can read submitted evidence; there is no private chat browser.

Message text is encrypted on your device. New photo/video attachments are not
end-to-end encrypted; avoid sending sensitive media. The relay sees accounts,
contact pairs, timestamps and sizes. Compare Verify keys through
another channel. This custom protocol has no forward secrecy or independent
audit. Video playback briefly uses a local decrypted MP4, deleted on close.

Portable and unsigned. The app checks the relay's HTTPS release manifest once
a day and verifies the download's size and SHA-256 before replacing itself.
Not affiliated with Sol's RNG or Telegram.
