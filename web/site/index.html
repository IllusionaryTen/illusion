Inside Illusion

Everything a conversation needs. Nothing it doesn't.

Built around the small things — quotes, timers, voice notes, auras — without giving up control of your words.

Sealed on your device

Messages, captions, replies and files are encrypted and signed on your device, then wrapped for each recipient with RSA-3072. The relay only ever forwards sealed envelopes.

Every screen you own

A portable Windows app that can update itself, a native Android shell that accepts shares from other apps, the browser app and a Telegram Mini App. One account, one network — the conversation continues where you left it.

Voice notes, sealed

Hold the mic to record up to 40 seconds. Recordings ride the same encrypted attachment pipeline as photos, videos and documents.

Timers that tidy up

Set a chat to disappear from one minute to 30 days. Either participant can change the timer; new messages follow it from the moment they land.

Open once

Send text, a photo or a video with View once. The recipient gets a sealed Open button, and the relay drops its copy after the first use.

Search, quotes and pins

Find any loaded message with in-chat search and step through hits with ↑/↓. Quote replies, react with a fixed emoji palette, pin chats, and mute a conversation locally — mute lists never leave your device.

Profiles with auras

Display name, description, fonts and colors — plus per-contact wallpapers, portable look files and ten aura themes that recolor the whole app.

Verified, device by device

Compare key fingerprints out of band and mark a contact verified. Review every signed-in device and sign any of them out instantly.

Security model

The relay forwards. It can't read.

Illusion keeps the keys on your devices. What travels is envelopes and routing information — never plaintext.

01

On your device

Text, captions, replies and file keys are sealed with AES-256-CBC plus an HMAC-SHA-256 tag, then wrapped with RSA-OAEP for each recipient.

02

On the relay

Sealed envelopes and public keys. The relay can see accounts, contact pairs, timestamps, sizes and IPs — it cannot open the bodies.

03

On their device

The receiver checks the signature and MAC, unwraps the content key with a private key that never left their device, and decrypts in memory.

Honest limits

What we don't claim

There is no forward secrecy or post-compromise recovery, and this custom protocol has not had an independent audit. A compromised device key exposes messages encrypted to it. Relay operators can retain ciphertext, and screenshots or modified clients can't be prevented.

Read the full security model

Cloudflare terminates TLS for the hosted relay, so Cloudflare sees connection metadata. There are no analytics, no remote fonts and no third-party scripts on the app or this site.

Get started

Four steps, then you're in.

  1. 01

    Pick a client

    Open the web app, install Windows or Android, or launch the Mini App from Telegram.

  2. 02

    Choose a name

    Usernames are 2–24 letters, digits, _ or - and are case-insensitive. Passwords need at least 10 characters.

  3. 03

    Save your key

    Download the .illusionkey backup when it's offered. It's the only way to read old messages on another device.

  4. 04

    Find someone

    Search the exact username, or start with Saved messages. Ctrl+K finds people, Ctrl+F searches a chat.

Your password resets the account, not the encryption. Without the device key or its backup, old chats can't be decrypted — by anyone. The preview is 18+ and limited to 1000 active accounts.

FAQ

Fair questions, straight answers.

Is Illusion free?

Yes. There are no payments, subscriptions, ads or marketing emails during the preview. The service is funded and hosted by its operator.

Do I need Telegram to use it?

No. Windows, Android and the web app work on their own. The bot @IllusedBot only delivers the Mini App, installers and connection files.

Can the operator read my chats?

Message text, captions, replies and files are sealed on your device before upload. The relay stores ciphertext, public keys and delivery metadata — who talked, when, how large, from which IP. It cannot turn that into readable messages without your keys. Notices from the host are explicitly not end-to-end encrypted.

What happens if I lose my key?

Your password cannot recreate it. Restore the .illusionkey backup or use a device that still has the key. Account recovery — a device-key challenge or a linked Gmail code — resets the password, but never restores missing history.

Which devices are supported?

Windows 10/11 with .NET Framework 4.8, Android 6.0+, any modern browser, and Telegram through the Mini App. The Windows app is portable and unsigned; the Android preview APK uses a debug key.

Can I edit or unsend messages?

No. Messages can't be edited or unsent. Disappearing timers remove content after their deadline, and View once removes it after the first open — but copies already made can't be recalled.

Is Illusion open source?

The source is public at github.com/IllusionaryTen/illusion. Administrative and moderation tooling is not published, and the hosted service keeps its own secrets.

How do I report someone?

Right-click an incoming message to report it, or use Profile / Timer to report or block. Reports go to a manual review queue, and blocking stops new messages in both directions.
