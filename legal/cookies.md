The Mini App sets no HTTP cookies and uses no advertising/analytics SDKs. Essential IndexedDB stores your account's non-extractable device encryption keys and public-key pins. Local storage holds appearance and acknowledgement preferences; the session token is memory-only. No chat plaintext is cached by this web client. Windows uses local settings/DPAPI files, not browser cookies.

The storage notice has equally accessible controls and no advertising consent bundled with sign-in. There are no optional trackers to accept or reject. You can clear this device's keys in Privacy and data after signing out; export a protected backup first. Clearing keys can make retained chats unreadable. Telegram and your browser manage their own storage outside this app. If optional cookies/tracking are introduced, they must remain disabled until an explicit opt-in, with an equally easy withdrawal control.
